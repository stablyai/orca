DEFECT FOUND BY EXERCISING THE RETRY (fixed, c2788f3672 and f481daad8d)
  The certification intent id is deterministic over its binding, so a retry
  re-derived the id the FAILED attempt had already consumed and the Task could
  never be picked up again. A retry now carries the Dispatch it retries in its
  binding and is verified as a retry; a retry grant will not authorise a first
  attempt and vice versa. Only exercising a real failure surfaced this.

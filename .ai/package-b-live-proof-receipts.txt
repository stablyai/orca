BLOCKER 8 FOLLOW-UP — runtime-owned evidence writers (commit 9847fac4a0)

Of the three kinds with no production writer, two are now closed and one is
blocked on something that does not exist in Orca:

  safe_launch_acceptance   CLOSED. worker-start makes this decision and used to
                           discard it; it is now persisted at the moment it is
                           made. A launch token is explicitly NOT accepted as a
                           substitute (negative control).

  effective_model_identity CLOSED. Read from the model the PROVIDER reports
  effective_reasoning_mode through its own hook, bound to this Dispatch's exact
                           pane, terminal and launch-token hash, and only when
                           the report is newer than the Dispatch. Reports from
                           another launch in the same pane, from a foreign pane
                           or terminal, or predating the Dispatch are all
                           refused (negative controls).

  pretool_acceptance       BLOCKED, and failing closed by design. It requires an
                           explicit accepted decision from an authoritative
                           PreTool policy path. Orca has no such path: managed
                           hook scripts emit a STATIC canned response
                           (src/main/cursor/hook-script.ts getCursorHookResponse
                           via wrapPosixHookCommand fallbackStdout), and a
                           repository-wide search finds no per-Dispatch PreTool
                           allow/deny decision anywhere in the main process —
                           the only references to such a decision are the
                           consumer added by this package.

                           The persistence and reader now exist, so the moment a
                           policy path records a decision it is consumed. Until
                           then this kind cannot be satisfied.

CONSEQUENCE, STATED PLAINLY
  A route still cannot reach PASS, so AUTO_BUILDER_TO_REVIEWER remains unproven.
  The remaining cause is no longer missing persistence — it is that Orca makes
  no per-Dispatch PreTool policy decision to persist. Creating one is new runtime
  policy, not an evidence writer, and inventing a proxy for it (a tool row, a
  PostToolUse event, a static hook response) is precisely the gaming path this
  package exists to remove and that I was corrected for attempting once already.

## Interpretation

Implementation and source regression coverage are not a device pass. The [device checklist](device-testing.md) stays pending until the corresponding action is observed. In particular, mocked relay handshakes prove protocol ordering, not access to the official relay service. Fixed E2EE vectors independently exercise the actual cryptographic code.

The official gateway limitation prevents a truthful claim of complete Android/iOS parity today. No remote-push registration is mislabelled as Android to bypass that boundary. A local socket notification is described as an online notification, not a replacement for terminated-application delivery.
